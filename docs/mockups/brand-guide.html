01

The name

W.A.G.E. expands to "We All Gotta Eat." Settled. Any copy reading "We All Gonna Eat" is wrong and gets corrected — including the Growth Playbook in Drive.

W.A.G.E. Society

Formal lockup. Headers, legal, first mention.

WAGE Society

Running text, URLs, casual reference.

WAGE

Product surfaces — WAGE World, WAGE Creator.

Tagline: We all gotta eat. Always ends with a period — it's a statement, not a slogan. Never "Wage Society", "WAGE society", or "The WAGE".

02

Positioning

WAGE Society is where creators own their audience and keep what they earn.
The problem we name

Platforms rent creators their own audience. Algorithms decide who gets heard, terms change overnight, and the split leaves crumbs.

What we actually are

A creator operating system — profile, streams, memberships, merch, referrals and Discord in one place, at 0% platform cut.

Not: a social network, a feed, or a growth hack. We don't promise reach. We promise ownership.

03

Voice

Talk like a creator who's done the work — not like a platform's marketing department.

01 · Plain over polished

If a word wouldn't survive being said out loud on a stream, cut it.

02 · Specific over hype

Numbers and nouns. Never "revolutionary" or "unlock your potential."

03 · Say the real number

Including zero. A zero that explains itself beats an invented count.

04 · Direct address

"Your profile", "you keep", "your people." Second person, active voice.

05 · Confident, never bitter

Name what platforms do wrong once, then move on to what we do instead. Ranting reads as small.

The correction that matters most

The cover images in docs/brand-assets/ are AI-generated marketing art, not brand assets. Version 1.0 of this guide took its palette from them and produced gold, cyan and violet with a glowing portal ring — none of which appears in the real logo. There is no cyan and no violet in this brand. Amber is both the brand and the primary action; the discipline is quantity, not hue — one amber button per view. Red means live, and nothing else.

Semantic colors stay separate and never borrow amber or red: #34D399 success · #E8A317 warning · #E5484D error. Amber text uses #F0900E at 16px+; base amber is for fills.

Why the mono face matters: it's the single strongest signal that WAGE is an operating system and not a brochure. Every figure a creator cares about — revenue, points, rank, member count, referral code — is set in mono with tabular numerals so columns line up. Numbers never get set in the body face.

06

The mark

Two real marks. The monogram signs the product; the crest carries the streaming presence.

Primary — the WS monogram
Secondary — the crest

The monogram is an interlocking W and S built from hard, flat, rounded-cap bars, mirrored across a horizontal rule with the wordmark letterspaced in the gap. It is monochrome by design and it is the mark in the nav, footer and favicon.

That construction — hard bars mirrored across a rule — is the brand's core visual device, and every graphic on the site should trace back to it. The homepage ledger animation is exactly that: bars descending from the rule as the mark's reflection.

The crest is the 3D badge whose W is two beer glasses over a red chevron. It spins on its vertical axis and belongs on Kick, Discord, overlays and merch — never as the site logo. It is where the palette comes from.

Clear space = the height of the WAGE SOCIETY band. Minimum 28px tall; below that, crop to the W/S bars. Never tint the monogram amber, add a glow, put it inside a circle or rounded-square badge, stretch either mark, or flatten the crest's bevel.

09

Motion & imagery

Motion

Motion belongs to the mark's mirror device. The ledger: hard-capped bars descend from the monogram's rule and rise and fall like an earnings chart or an audio meter — silver by default, amber every sixth column, one red column travelling across as the live marker. Square caps only. Transitions 120–200ms ease-out. No glows, no pulsing rings, no scroll parallax. The crest's own spin is the system's only rotation, and it stays on streaming surfaces. prefers-reduced-motion renders one static frame.

Imagery

Blue-black ground, one warm amber light source, red only where something is live. Hard geometry over soft glow — the marks are flat and extruded, not luminous. Never bright backgrounds, business stock, generic gradient meshes, or the AI cover art in docs/brand-assets/. Creator avatars and merch photos are the exception — those are theirs.
